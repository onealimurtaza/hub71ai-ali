# Three-minute demo — no slides

## 0:00–0:20: The problem

A family relocation is several linked journeys. An employer may handle one adult's permit, while their partner needs business permissions, their child needs a nursery and their parents need separate sponsorship and health cover. Arrival brings those decisions into one practical plan.

## 0:20–1:15: Start the conversation

Start fresh. Type or dictate:

“I live in Germany and have a German passport. I want to move to Abu Dhabi in two months with my wife and our three-year-old child. I have a software engineering job offer. My wife wants to start an online business. We have an approximate monthly living budget of AED 18,000. We may bring my elderly parents later, so insurance is important.”

In AI mode, demonstrate that the profile captures multiple details without asking the same questions again. Answer any remaining question honestly. Do not substitute a scripted example for evidence of a working AI response. If the service falls back, explain that the app is using its guided mode.

## 1:15–1:45: Review the profile

Show the household, employer offer, partner's business plans, child's age, timing and priorities. Correct a detail if necessary. Generate the plan.

## 1:45–2:35: Show value

Show temporary accommodation, employer responsibilities, the partner's business route, family/parent residence and insurance, and nursery planning. Open an official source. Explain that checkmarks track personal progress and that requirements are confirmed with authorities.

## 2:35–3:00: Close

Show the budget worksheet and the live URL. Explain that OpenAI understands the situation and chooses missing questions, while reviewed rules provide the planning steps. Mention the GitHub repository and exact submitted SHA only after they exist and are verified.
